# Legal review checklist (TICK-163)

This draft supports TICK-163. It is not a legal sign-off.

## Current public sources checked

- Eswatini Competition Commission consumer protection page: emphasizes respect, transparency and fair treatment for consumers. https://www.compco.co.sz/service/consumer-protection/
- Eswatini Fair Trading Act, 2001: prohibits misleading or deceptive conduct, including misleading conduct about goods or services. https://www.compco.co.sz/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Fair-Trading-Act-2001.pdf
- Eswatini Competition Act, 2007: establishes competition-law objectives including consumer welfare. https://investeswatini.org.sz/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/COMPETITION-ACT-2007.pdf

## Review questions for counsel

- Confirm whether the refund, postponement and no-show clauses are enforceable for event tickets sold in Eswatini.
- Confirm whether platform fees, payment-provider fees or organizer-settlement recovery can be excluded from refunds, and what disclosures are required before checkout.
- Confirm required wording for organizer cancellations, material event changes and delayed provider refunds.
- Confirm whether ticket resale and transfer restrictions need additional consumer disclosures.
- Confirm whether liability limitations are appropriately scoped and do not exclude non-excludable rights.
- Confirm the correct governing-law and dispute-resolution language for Ticketiv's launch entity.

## Product follow-ups after legal review

- Add version dates if policy approval requires immutable snapshots.
- Confirm checkout acceptance copy matches the final legal wording.
- Add organizer-side policy authoring guidance if event-specific refund terms need stronger validation.
